## Highlights

A revision of 0.1.7: Settings now goes **back with your mouse's back button**, and the **download progress on the Updates page** no longer redraws the page.

### ⬅️ Back and forward in Settings

- **Mouse:** the back button returns to the page you were on before; the forward button goes forward again.
- **Keyboard:** Alt+Left and Alt+Right do the same, as in Windows' own Settings.

### ⬇️ A steady download on the Updates page

While an update downloaded, the Updates page was built anew for each percent, so it flickered and its spinning ring started over. Now only the progress bar moves. The fix is in 0.1.7 and later; the download of an update is shown by the version you have installed, so you will see the change from the next update on.
