## Highlights

Deskboxes now works on **every monitor**: each screen gets its own boxes and pages. Game boxes can show **covers**, and you can **move everything to another PC** with one file. Settings has a **search box**, and **Narrator** can read your boxes and icons. This release also fixes updates that stayed at "downloading 100%".

### 🖥️ Boxes on every monitor

Each screen now has its own boxes and its own desktop pages. Drag an icon or a box from one screen to another the same way you move them on one screen. If you unplug a monitor, its boxes and icons move to your main screen, on a page of their own, and a notice tells you where they went. Plug it back in and they return to it. Swipes and screen edges switch the pages of the screen they happen on (**Settings › Desktop pages**).

### 🎮 Covers for your games

A box can now show its games as covers, like a game library: right-click a box › **View › Covers**. Steam games use the art Steam already keeps on your PC, so nothing is downloaded. Other games, and Steam games without art yet, get a cover-sized tile with their icon. The Games box that **Make boxes** creates now starts in Covers.

### 📦 Move to another PC

**Settings › Saved layouts › Move to another PC › Export…** saves your boxes, pages, sorting rules, saved layouts and settings, for every screen, in one `.deskboxes` file. On the new PC, choose **Import…** and pick the file. Deskboxes saves your current layout under Saved layouts first, then restarts with the imported one. Icons are matched by name, so the boxes fill up even though the files are new copies.

### 🧩 Other notable changes

- **Settings:** a **Find a setting** box at the top finds any setting by its name or description, opens its page and highlights it.
- **Narrator:** screen readers can read the boxes by their titles and the icons in them, and follow the focus as you move it with the keyboard. **Tab**, **Shift+Tab** and **F6** jump between boxes.
- **Updates:** Settings now shows "Version … is ready" as soon as the download finishes. Before, it could stay at "downloading 100%" until you reopened Settings.

## Full release notes

### Accessibility

* Added a UI Automation tree for screen readers: boxes are groups named by their titles, their icons are list items, and the focused icon is announced
* Added **Tab**, **Shift+Tab** and **F6** to move the keyboard focus to the next or previous box

### Boxes

* Added the **Covers** view, with Steam's local cover art and cover-sized icon tiles for other games
* Changed the Games box from **Make boxes** to start in Covers

### Icons

* Fixed some folders showing a black square behind their icon; folder pictures that Windows hands over without see-through corners now fall back to the plain folder icon. Icons are read again once after updating.

### Multiple Monitors

* Added boxes and desktop pages on every screen
* Added dragging icons and boxes between screens
* Added keeping an unplugged screen's boxes and icons on the main screen, with a notice saying where they are, and moving them back when the screen returns
* Fixed the boxes from **Make boxes** being empty when the pointer was on another screen during the first start

### Settings

* Added **Find a setting** at the top of the navigation
* Added **Move to another PC** (Export and Import) under **Saved layouts**
* Fixed the window being resizable to any size; it now keeps a minimum size
* Fixed **Look** staying selected in the navigation after going to another page

### Updates

* Fixed Settings staying at "downloading 100%" after the download finished, until it was reopened

### Known issues

* If you still see a black square behind a folder after this update, please [report it](https://github.com/deskboxes/deskboxes/issues/new/choose) with a screenshot.
* Covers come only from Steam's local cache. Games from other stores show a tile with their icon.
* Importing on another PC matches icons by name. Icons that were renamed in between stay where Deskboxes would put a new icon.
