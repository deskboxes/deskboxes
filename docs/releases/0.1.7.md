## Highlights

Deskboxes **starts faster on slower PCs**, and the **desktop menu keeps working** instead of stopping until a restart. Explorer's own icons no longer show when a PC is slow to start. Settings' **Home page shows your desktop**, with your boxes on your wallpaper and counts that take you where you need to go.

### 🚀 Faster on slower PCs

The first picture of your desktop comes up about twice as fast. On a PC throttled to a fraction of its speed, it took about a second; now it takes about half that.

- **Icon labels:** their shadows are made in one go instead of one by one. That took 112 ms on our test PC and 10 ms now; on the throttled PC, 610 ms and now 10 ms.
- **Wallpaper:** it is kept ready from one start to the next. Decoding it took up to 0.9 s at sign-in; now it is read back in a few milliseconds.
- **At sign-in:** if the graphics card or the desktop isn't ready yet, Deskboxes waits for it instead of giving up.

### 🖱️ A desktop menu that keeps working

Right-clicking could stop showing a menu until Deskboxes was restarted. The menu runs in a helper program. After five helper restarts within a minute, Deskboxes stopped starting it for good, and a menu that took more than 8 seconds on a busy PC got its helper ended.

- Deskboxes now waits 30 seconds and tries again, however often the helper had to restart.
- The helper reports as it builds a menu, so a slow menu is shown instead of cut off.
- A right-click while no helper is running starts one and then shows the menu.
- After sleep, the helper starts afresh, and a menu that opened on no screen is shown again.

### 🖥️ Explorer's icons stay hidden on slow starts

When a PC was slow to start, Deskboxes could take a while to draw its first frame. The watchdog took that for a hang, put Explorer's icons back and restarted Deskboxes. It now gives a starting Deskboxes 90 seconds, and after that needs two missed checks in a row. Hiding Explorer's icons is also retried if Explorer isn't ready.

### 🏠 Settings: your desktop on Home

- **Your desktop:** a map of this page's boxes, in their colors, on your wallpaper. Click a box to change its name and look.
- **Counts:** your boxes, icons, desktop pages and sorting rules. Click one to open its page.
- **Feature tiles** show how many sorting rules, saved layouts and folder boxes you have.
- **Shortcuts** sit two to a row, with the keys under what they do.

### 🧩 Other notable changes

- **Settings:** scrolling uses Windows' own scrolling, so the wheel, touchpad and keyboard stay smooth while the page is busy.
- **Settings:** opens sooner after Deskboxes starts, and Home draws before the tiles further down.
- **Speed:** many smaller improvements to sorting, search, dragging, drawing and memory use. The full list is below.

## Full release notes

### Context menu

* Fixed right-clicks showing no menu until Deskboxes was restarted, after the menu helper had restarted five times within a minute
* Fixed a slow menu on a busy PC being cut off after 8 seconds
* Fixed a right-click while the menu helper wasn't running showing nothing
* Fixed the menu not showing after the PC woke from sleep
* Fixed an old menu helper's messages being taken for the new one's
* Fixed right-clicks being ignored after some page gestures
* Changed the menu helper to run at normal priority

### Desktop

* Fixed Explorer's icons showing, and Deskboxes restarting, when a PC was slow to start
* Fixed Explorer's icons showing when Deskboxes started before the graphics card or the desktop was ready
* Fixed hiding Explorer's icons not being retried when Explorer wasn't ready
* Improved the first frame at sign-in: icon label shadows are made together, and the wallpaper is kept ready between starts

### Performance

* Improved sorting with many rules: icons are moved together
* Improved typing in search: less work for each key
* Improved file changes: only the folder that changed is read again
* Improved drawing: screens and areas that didn't change aren't drawn again
* Improved dragging and rubber-band selection with many icons
* Improved memory use: recolored icons are kept within a size limit
* Improved the first start: icons show before all their previews are made
* Improved Narrator support: the list of boxes and icons is updated only when it changes
* Reduced how often the watchdog wakes up

### Settings

* Added a map of your desktop and counts of boxes, icons, pages and sorting rules on Home
* Added counts to the Sorting rules, Saved layouts and Folder boxes tiles
* Added names for Narrator to Home's tiles and switches
* Changed Home's shortcuts to two columns
* Changed a tile's clip to also play when the tile is reached with the keyboard
* Changed scrolling to Windows' own, smooth on the wheel, touchpad and keyboard
* Improved how soon Settings opens after Deskboxes starts, and how soon Home is drawn
* Improved tile clips: each is loaded when you first point at it
