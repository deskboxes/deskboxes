## Highlights

**Search** opens into a list of what it found, can hide every icon that doesn't match, and can make a box of the results. Explorer's own icons no longer flash on the desktop after you sign in. In Settings, the feature clips play again, scrolling is smooth, and changing a setting keeps your place on the page.

### 🔍 A new search

Start typing on the desktop, or press **Ctrl+F**, and the search field opens into a **list of results**: each icon with its name and where it is. Click one to show it, double-click to open it. Chips at the top filter by type (Folders, Documents, Pictures, Games, Apps and more) and show how many there are; typing `pdf:` limits a search to that file type. Matching forgives a typo or two and ranks the best matches first.

- **Hide the others** hides every icon that doesn't match. The matches in each box close up, and the switch is remembered for your next search.
- **Make a box of these** puts this page's matches in a new box. **Move to a box…** moves them into one you already have.
- The field can sit at the **bottom** of the screen, with the list opening upward, or at the **top**, with the list dropping down (**Settings › Search**).

### 🖥️ No more Explorer icons at sign-in

Explorer's own desktop icons could show for a moment after you signed in, restarted or updated, until Deskboxes started. Windows ends Deskboxes when you sign out, and Deskboxes took that for a crash and turned Explorer's icons back on. It now tells the two apart, so the icons stay off.

### 🧩 Other notable changes

- **Settings:** the feature clips play again. Hover over a tile on Home, or open a feature's page, to watch it. They didn't play at all in 0.1.5 and earlier.
- **Settings:** changing a setting no longer takes you back to the top of the page. Sorting rules is one example.
- **Settings:** the mouse wheel scrolls smoothly, a notch about as far as in Windows Settings. The gaps between cards scroll too.
- **Folder previews:** a folder that fell back to the plain folder icon now gets its preview once Windows has made it.

## Full release notes

### Desktop

* Fixed Explorer's desktop icons showing after sign-in, a restart or an update until Deskboxes started. Ending the session no longer counts as a crash.

### Icons

* Fixed folders that fell back to the plain folder icon keeping it. They are asked for again after 10 seconds, 30 seconds, 2 minutes and 10 minutes.

### Search

* Added a list of results with type filter chips and counts; one click shows an icon, a double-click opens it
* Added **Hide the others**, which hides icons that don't match and closes up the matches in each box
* Added **Make a box of these** and **Move to a box…** for this page's matches
* Added `ext:` filters, such as `pdf:`
* Added ranked matching that forgives one or two typos
* Added a field at the top of the screen as a second style
* Added Settings for the list, the switch and where the field is

### Settings

* Fixed the feature clips never playing, on Home tiles and at the top of each feature's page
* Fixed the clips starting slightly off size and looking softer than their pictures; tiles have their own clips now
* Fixed changing a setting rebuilding the page and taking you back to its top
* Changed the mouse wheel to scroll smoothly, about as far a notch as Windows Settings, also over the gaps between cards
* Changed the Search picture and clip to show the new search
