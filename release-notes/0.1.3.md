## Desktop pages
- More desktops side by side, as on a phone's home screen. Each page has its own boxes and icons; your files stay in the Desktop folder.
- Go from page to page with Alt+Left or Alt+Right, Alt and the mouse wheel, a two-finger swipe on a touchpad, or by dragging the desktop with the middle button or with both buttons held. Pages follow your hand and slide into place.
- Hold icons or a box at the left or right edge of the screen to take them to the next page. Past the last page, a new one is made.
- The page's name shows at the top of the screen while you switch, with a dot for each page. Double-click it to rename the page, right-click it for the pages menu.
- Make, rename and delete pages from the desktop menu (Deskboxes › Pages) or in Settings › Desktop pages. A deleted page's boxes and icons move to the page next to it, and Ctrl+Z brings the page back.
- Move a box or selected icons to another page from their menu.
- Choose where new files appear: on the page you're on, or on the first page.
- Search finds icons on every page and takes you to the right one.
- Every switching method can be turned off, and so can the whole feature.

## Fixes
- Turning Deskboxes off now leaves "Turn on Deskboxes" in Windows 11's own menu, not only under "Show more options".
- After turning Deskboxes off and on again, the right-click menu works straight away.
- With boxes above windows (Win+Shift+D), you can drag icons from a box into an app, between boxes and onto the desktop. Win+Shift+D does nothing while the desktop itself is in front.
- Dragging a rectangle with the right mouse button now selects icons inside a box too.
