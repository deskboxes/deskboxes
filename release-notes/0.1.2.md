## Files, like in Explorer
- Undo and redo (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y) now also work for moving icons into a folder, sending them to the Recycle Bin and renaming.
- Drag icons the way you do in Explorer. The icon follows the pointer, a note says what will happen ("Move to Recycle Bin", "Open with…"), and folders light up under it.
- Drop files on the Recycle Bin to delete them. Shift+Delete asks first, in Windows' own dialog.
- New files and folders appear where you right-clicked, ready to rename.
- Drag a rectangle with the right mouse button to select icons and open their menu.
- Drop an icon between two others to put it right there, on the desktop and in boxes.

## Boxes
- Each box can have its own icon size and view: extra large to small icons, List, Details or Tiles. Hold Ctrl and scroll over a box to change it, or set all boxes at once from the desktop's View menu.
- A tab's color now colors its box while that tab is open.
- Folder boxes can show any folder you choose, and you can change it later. They now have tabs too.
- Boxes above windows: the wallpaper no longer turns black, and nothing peeks out under the taskbar.
- Icon tint keeps each icon's light and shade. Blend into wallpaper gives icons and their names the wallpaper's colors until you point at them.

## Sorting
- On a new install, your icons are sorted into boxes for apps, folders, documents, media, and installers & archives. Ctrl+Z puts them back.
- With no rules yet, "Sort now" makes those boxes for you.
- A selection rectangle started on the desktop now goes behind the boxes.

## Smoother
- Dragging, selecting and boxes with many files are quicker, most of all on older PCs.
- Deskboxes starts right after you sign in, without the usual startup delay.
- Changing the wallpaper shows up at once.
- The desktop menu opens where Windows' own menu would, and it closes when you click into another app.

## Settings
- New pages for Blend into wallpaper, Updates and About.
- Every feature has a new picture. Point at it to see the feature at work. On a feature's own page it plays large.
- Light and dark mode match everywhere, including file dialogs.
